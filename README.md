## 👋 Hi, I'm Jacob Tender

I'm a software developer and writer living in Ohio. My interests mainly surround metadata and archival projects. 

I have a [website](https://jacobtender.net/) detailing many of them.

## 🔭 I’m currently working on...

### [Hardcover](https://github.com/hardcoverapp) - Social discovery for serious book lovers

As a Senior Librarian on the [Hardcover](https://hardcover.app/join?referrer_id=8753) Team, I serve as a community leader and technical contributor. I guide policies and practices around book data quality, striving for metadata accuracy and strong processes.

### [Marian](https://github.com/jacobtender/marian-extension) - A book data assistant

Marian the Librarian helps Hardcover.app contributors and other online book data enthusiasts view and capture book metadata from popular retail and catalog sites. With a single click, the sidebar pulls down titles, contributors, identifiers, descriptions, cover art, and more so you can manage clean data without endlessly scrolling, copying, and pasting.

Site support includes: Amazon, Kobo, Goodreads, The Storygraph, Google Books, ISBN Search, and more.

### [Hardcover for TRMNL](https://github.com/jacobtender/hardcover_trmnl)

A [TRMNL](https://usetrmnl.com/?ref=jacob15) plugin for [Hardcover](https://hardcover.app/join?referrer_id=8753) users to display account statistics and goals, as well as books recently read, currently reading, or those on those ever-growing too-be-read pile.
